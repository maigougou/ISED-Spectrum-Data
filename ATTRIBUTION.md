# Data provenance

Source: **Terrestrial Spectrum Licence Site Data Extract**, Innovation, Science and Economic Development Canada (ISED).

- Download page: https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/spectrum-management-system/en/spectrum-management-system-data/download-sms-data
- Official ZIP: https://www.ic.gc.ca/engineering/SMS_TAFL_Files/Site_Data_Extract_FX.zip
- Export field definitions: https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/spectrum-management-system/sites/default/files/documents/Field%20Descriptions%20-%20Descriptions%20des%20champs_0.pdf
- ISED usage terms: https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/ised/en/terms-and-conditions

ISED Spectrum Management System Data is an independent community data project. It is not produced in affiliation with, or endorsed by, the Government of Canada or a carrier. The original government publication remains the source of record. These files project and index selected factual source fields; generated names, identifiers, groupings and frequency-band candidates are this project's interpretation, not official source assertions.

The raw source is not redistributed here. Contact names, teleclient numbers, email addresses, upload references and unrelated administrative fields are excluded. Original reported technology, frequencies, coordinates, technical parameters and source identifiers are retained where needed. Invalid rows are excluded and counted in each quality report.

Reported locations and antenna directions are not measured cellular coverage, speeds, live service availability, or proof of access by a particular retail brand. Multiple frequency rows do not mean multiple towers. Locations are grouped only by verbatim source licensee and normalized coordinates, not by approximate proximity. The account and record ID fields are excluded in schema v1. See FIELD_SELECTION.md for the exact projection.

Export units are MHz (both frequency and bandwidth), metres (height), degrees (angles) and percent (downlink allocation). Frequency-band candidates follow the operating-band ranges in 3GPP TS 36.101 and TS 38.101-1. Overlapping candidates are kept and technology is never inferred from frequency alone.

This notice concerns data provenance; publishing the processing code does not relicense government source materials.
